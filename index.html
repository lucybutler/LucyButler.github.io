About Me

Hello! I’m Lucy Butler, a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Psychology of Misinformation Lab at the Network Science Institute at Northeastern University.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Network Science Institute, Northeastern University, specialising in how social media information environments influence misinformation susceptibility and misinformation intervention efficacy. My work uses innovative experimental methods to examine how individuals encounter, evaluate, and update beliefs in response to false information, and the downstream consequences of such beliefs on important societal outcomes (e.g., decisions to vaccinate).
